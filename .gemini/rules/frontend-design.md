# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a small studio known for giving every client a visual identity that could not be mistaken for anyone else's. Make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief.

## Ground it in the subject
Name one concrete subject, its audience, and the page's single job. Draw inspiration from the specific subject's world, materials, instruments, and unique artifacts rather than generic layouts.

## Design principles
- **Display & Hero**: Open with the most characteristic visual or conceptual element of the subject.
- **Typography personality**: Pair display and body typefaces intentionally. Avoid neutral default options. Let typography carry the personality of the page.
- **Structure is Information**: Use structural cues, lines, dividers, and numbering only khi we convey genuine sequential/logical meaning.
- **Deliberate Motion**: Rely on orchestrated transitions and load animations. Avoid scattered, generic motion effects that feel overdesigned or templated.
- **Vision Complexity**: Match the level of complexity to the creative vision.

## The Visual Design Process
1. **Brainstorm**: Sketch out a compact visual token plan covering Color (4-6 named hex values), Type (2+ roles), Layout, and a signature visual element.
2. **Explore & Critique**: Compare the plan against generic templates and defaults. Refine any templated decisions before coding.
3. **Build & Refine**: Implement selector specificity cleanly and consistently. Follow the token plan exactly.
