# Communication Guide
## Nhan yeu cau tu user
- Khi da ro: Neu lai hieu biet ngan gon (1-2 cau) truoc khi lam.
- Khi chua ro: Gom cau hoi lai hoi 1 lan ro rang, giai thich ly do can biet.
- Phat hien van de rong hon: Hoi y kien nguoi dung truoc khi thuc hien.
## Trong qua trinh lam
- Bao cao progress: Cap nhat tien do doi voi task lon.
- Khi cach tiep can khong chay: Dung som, de xuat cac phuong an thay the kem trade-off cu the.
## Bao cao ket qua
- Format chuan: Neu ro Da lam gi, Thay doi o nhung files nao, Cach kiem thu (test) va cac Luu y dac biet.
- Estimate thuc te: Khong hua hen qua ngan, uoc luong kem bien do an toan.
- Giai quyet Scope Creep: Dat lai ky vong ro rang khi xuat hien yeu cau moi ngoai scope ban dau.
